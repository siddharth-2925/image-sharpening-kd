Image Quality Study

You will view 20 pairs of native-resolution image crops. For each pair, rate the visual quality and detail of A and B separately from 1 (very poor) to 5 (excellent). Look at sharpness, readable detail, and artifacts. If A and B look equally good, give them the same rating. You do not need to guess which method made each image. This takes about 10 minutes.

Do not enter your name. Use a different ID for each participant.
